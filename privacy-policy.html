Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 June 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how privacy works in Fish Finger, developed by Daniel Lang.

Contact: danjonlang91@hotmail.com

1. Summary

Fish Finger does not have user accounts, sign-up, profiles, usernames, chat, leaderboards, or social features. We do not directly collect your name, email, phone number, address, or payment details.

The game stores only basic local data on your device: your pearl balance, upgrade levels, game settings, and local progress. This stays on your device and is not sent to us.

Fish Finger shows ads through Google AdMob, which may collect device and usage data to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalised advertising.

2. Data we collect ourselves

We do not directly collect personal data through Fish Finger. There is no account system, registration, email sign-up, user profile, in-game chat, or direct collection of personal details by us.

If you email us for support, we will use your email only to respond to you and keep appropriate records.

3. Local save data

Fish Finger stores game data locally on your device (pearl balance, upgrade levels, settings, progress). We do not receive it or store it on our servers. If you delete the game, clear app data, change device, or reinstall, your local save data may be lost and we may not be able to restore it.

4. Advertising through Google AdMob

Fish Finger uses Google AdMob to show ads. AdMob may collect device identifiers, advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions, ad views, diagnostic information, and other technical data. AdMob may use this to show ads, measure performance, prevent fraud, and provide personalised or non-personalised ads depending on your settings, region, and consent choices.

We do not control what data Google collects through AdMob. Google is responsible for its own privacy practices.

Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy
Google Ads Privacy Info: policies.google.com/technologies/ads

5. Rewarded ads and pearls

When you choose to watch a rewarded ad, AdMob may process information about the ad request, display, and completion. Pearls are in-game currency only — they have no real-world value and cannot be exchanged for cash or goods.

6. Personalised advertising

Depending on your location, device settings, and consent choices, AdMob may show personalised or non-personalised ads. You may be able to limit or reset ad personalisation through your Android device settings or Google account settings.

If you are located in the European Economic Area, United Kingdom, or Switzerland, you will be shown a consent form when you first launch the app, allowing you to choose whether personalised ads are shown to you.

7. Children

Fish Finger is intended for players aged 13 and over. If you believe a child under 13 has provided personal data, contact us at danjonlang91@hotmail.com.

8. Third-party links and ads

Ads may link to third-party apps or websites. We are not responsible for their privacy practices. Review the relevant third party's policy if you click an ad.

9. Data sharing

We do not sell your personal data or directly share it with advertisers. Google may collect and process data through AdMob as described above.

10. Data retention

Local game data remains on your device until you delete the app or clear app data. Support emails may be kept as long as reasonably needed. Data collected by AdMob is handled by Google according to their policies.

11. Your choices and rights

You can delete local game data by deleting Fish Finger or clearing app data in device settings. You can manage ad personalisation through Android device settings and any consent choices shown in the app. Depending on where you live, you may have legal rights over your personal data — contact us if you have questions.

12. Security

We do not operate a user account database for Fish Finger. Local save data is stored on your device and you are responsible for keeping your device secure. No system is completely secure.

13. International users

Fish Finger may be available in different countries. If you use Fish Finger outside the United Kingdom, privacy rules may differ from those in your country.

14. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Your continued use of Fish Finger after an update means you accept the updated policy.
